Dahi Ke Kabab

“Crisp outside, melt within.”

Delicate hung-yogurt kababs, crisp without, molten within

⏱ Prep time
20 min + chill
🔥 Cook time
15 min
🍽 Serves
3–4
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Hung yogurt – 3 cups (should be really thick)
  • Grated paneer
  • Onion, chopped – 1 medium
  • Ginger, chopped – 1 inch piece
  • Green chillies, chopped – 3–4
  • Turmeric powder – 1/2 tsp
  • Red chilli powder – 1 tsp
  • Garam masala powder – 1 tsp
  • Dry fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) – 1/2 tbsp
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped – 2 tbsp
  • Cornflour – 2 tbsp + 1/2 tbsp for rolling
  • Oil to deep / shallow fry

Method

1

Take hung yogurt in a bowl. Add finely chopped onion, ginger, green chillies and grated paneer, turmeric powder, red chilli powder, garam masala powder, kasuri methi, salt and coriander leaves. Mix in 2 tbsp cornflour and 1/2 tbsp maida and mix well. Keep the mixture in the fridge for 15–20 minutes so that it hardens — this is important, else it will be semi-solid and won't hold.

💡 Pro Tip: Chill the mixture till it firms up before shaping — skip this and the kababs stay semi-solid and won’t hold together.
2

Divide into small portions. Dampen your hands, take a portion, roll lightly in cornflour and shape into a kebab (one centimetre thick round). Prepare the other kebabs the same way. Sprinkle rava on top — this gives the crispiness.

3

Heat sufficient oil in a kadai and deep-fry the kebabs till golden. Or shallow-fry on a lightly greased tava until golden brown, turning to cook the other side.

4

Remove and place onto absorbent paper. Serve hot with a chutney, sauce or achari dip.
