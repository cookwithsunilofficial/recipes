I…Scream for Ice-Cream

“Summer in a scoop.”

Bombay-style naturals, churned by hand at home

▶ Watch the show 60-sec recipe reel
⏱ Prep time
30 min
🔥 Cook time
Freeze 8–10 hrs
🍽 Serves
3 flavours
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian

Ingredients

Ice Cream Base

  • Whipped cream – 1 cup
  • Condensed milk – 1/2 cup

For Mango Ice Cream

  • 1 crushed mango pulp
  • 1 mango chopped into pieces
  • A pinch of yellow food colour (optional)

For Chocolate Ice Cream

  • Melted chocolate compound
  • Choc chips as desired

For Vanilla Ice Cream

  • Vanilla essence – 1 tsp

Method

1

I've used whipping cream powder — to make cream from the powder, follow the instructions on the packet. You need two packets to prepare 1 cup of cream.

2

Take 100 ml of cold milk (5°C) per packet of whipping cream powder. Add the powder and milk and beat with a beater. An electric beater is preferable — 3–4 minutes — versus 10–15 minutes by hand. You can also use ready whipping cream from the market; in that case skip the milk and just beat the cream for 3–4 minutes until it thickens. Don't use Amul cream — it's thin, with 25% fat.

3

Once the cream becomes thick, add the condensed milk and beat again for 30 seconds. Your ice cream base is ready.

4

Split the mixture into 3 different air-tight containers. In the first, add vanilla essence and mix well.

5

In the second container add mango pulp and mix well, then add mango pieces on top. For a more yellow colour, add a pinch of yellow food colour and mix well.

6

For chocolate ice cream, take the third container and add the melted dark chocolate. Mix well, then sprinkle in loads of choc chips and mix again.

7

Cover with an air-tight lid and keep in the refrigerator for 2 hours. After 2 hours, take a spoon or spatula and gently mix so that no crushed ice forms. Freeze again for 8–10 hours and your yummy ice cream is ready. The same way, you can make paan, sapota, or tender coconut — any flavour of your choice.
