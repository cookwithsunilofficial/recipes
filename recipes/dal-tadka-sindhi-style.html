Dal Tadka – Sindhi Style

“Comfort in a bowl.”

Chana dal, gently spiced, finished with a sizzling tadka

⏱ Prep time
45 min
🔥 Cook time
20 min
🍽 Serves
3–4
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Split Bengal gram (chana dal) – 1 cup
  • Salt to taste
  • Turmeric powder – 1/4 tsp
  • Red chilli powder – 1/2 tsp
  • Garam masala powder – 1/4 tsp
  • Dry mango powder (amchur) – 1/2 tsp
  • Oil – 3 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds (jeera) – 1 tsp
  • Green chillies, slit long – 2–3
  • Curry leaves – 8–10
  • Onion, chopped – 1 medium
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped – 1/2 cup

Method

1

Soak chana dal in three cups of water for about 30–45 minutes. Drain and boil with 3 cups of water, salt and turmeric powder till just done. Preferably don't use the cooker — it should be just boiled enough.

2

In a pan, add the boiled dal and half a cup of water if the dal is too dry. Stir and add half the red chilli powder, half the garam masala powder, dry mango powder and adjust salt. Stir gently and cook on low heat.

💡 Pro Tip: Skip the pressure cooker — dal boiled just-done keeps its bite instead of turning to mush.
3

Heat oil in another pan and add cumin seeds. When they begin to change colour, add green chillies, curry leaves, the remaining garam masala powder and remaining red chilli powder.

4

Stir and add it to the dal. Mix well and take it off the heat.

5

Garnish with onion and coriander leaves and serve hot.
