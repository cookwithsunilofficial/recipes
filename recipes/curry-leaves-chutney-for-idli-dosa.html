Curry Leaves Chutney for Idli / Dosa

“Your dosa’s best friend.”

The everyday companion to idli and dosa, fragrant and green

⏱ Prep time
5 min
🔥 Cook time
10 min
🍽 Serves
3–4
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1–2 green chilli
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp jeera
  • 1 tsp Bengal gram dal
  • 1 cup curry leaves
  • 1 piece of ginger
  • 4–5 garlic cloves
  • Salt as desired

Method

1

Take oil in a kadai.

2

Once the oil is hot, add jeera and roast a bit, then add Bengal gram and roast, then add chilli, ginger and garlic and roast them all together.

3

Add curry leaves and roast a bit.

4

Grind all the ingredients in a mixer with the required quantity of water, based on the consistency you want, and garnish with tadka.

✨ Serving Tip: Keeps well in the fridge in an airtight jar for about a week.
