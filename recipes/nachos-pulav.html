Nachos Pulav

“Where Mumbai meets Mexico.”

Layered rice where Mumbai street food meets a Mexican crunch

⏱ Prep time
4–10 hrs (rajma soak)
🔥 Cook time
25 min
🍽 Serves
2–3
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup boiled rajma (soaked 3–4 hrs, pressure-cooked 8–10 whistles)
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (boiled sweet corn, chopped capsicum, chopped onion)
  • 5–6 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 cup tomato puree (2–3 tomatoes, ground)
  • 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • Salt as desired
  • Crushed nachos (15–20 pieces)
  • 2 tsp tomato ketchup
  • 5 tsp ghee / olive oil

Method

1

Heat 3 tsp of ghee (out of 5) in a pan, add the tomato puree and simmer till the oil separates. Keep the puree aside.

2

Take another pan, add the remaining 2 tsp ghee and fry the garlic.

3

Add onion and cook for 2 minutes, then add the rest of the vegetables and cook for 3–4 minutes.

4

Now add the tomato mixture from Step 1.

5

Add the tomato ketchup.

6

Add the salt, oregano and chilli flakes and mix well.

7

Take a pan, spread some ghee and add a layer of rice.

💡 Pro Tip: Building it in layers, rather than mixing everything together, is what gives each spoonful pockets of masala instead of an even, muted flavour throughout.
8

Now add the prepared masala mixture on top of the rice.

9

Keep making 2–3 layers like this.

10

Heat in a microwave for 30 seconds and serve hot. No oven? Put the pulav in a steel vessel, place it inside a kadai with water, cover with a lid, and steam for 1 minute.

11

Sprinkle the crushed nachos on top just before serving, and your garama-garam Nachos Pulav is ready. What are you waiting for?

✨ Serving Tip: Add the nachos at the very last moment — any earlier and they’ll turn soft from the steam.
