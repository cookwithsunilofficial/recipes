Flat Bread Vegetable Stir Fry (Kuttu Roti)

“Leftovers never tasted this good.”

Leftover rotis, crisped in ghee and tossed with spiced vegetables

⏱ Prep time
15 min
🔥 Cook time
20 min
🍽 Serves
3–4
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 10–12 rotis, cut into small squares
  • Butter / ghee for frying
  • 3 onions, chopped
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup capsicum / bell pepper (red/yellow/green), small cut
  • 2 tbsp pav bhaji masala
  • A bit of garam masala
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • Other vegetables of choice (boiled beans, green peas, etc.)
  • Coriander leaves, to garnish
  • Shredded cheese / pomegranate / chopped tomatoes, to top
  • 2–3 green chillies, long cut (optional, for spice)

Method

1

Cut around 10–12 rotis into small squares. Add some butter/ghee to a kadai and shallow-fry till crispy. Set aside.

2

In a saucepan, add some oil. Once hot, add 3 chopped onions and sauté till lightly browned. Add ginger-garlic paste, 2 chopped tomatoes, 1 cup small-cut capsicum and stir well.

3

Add 2 tbsp pav bhaji masala, a bit of garam masala, half a lemon's juice and any other chopped vegetables of your choice, like boiled beans or green peas. Cook, stirring lightly at intervals, till all the vegetables are cooked.

4

Add the fried roti pieces, mix well, and let it simmer for a few minutes. Remove from the fire and garnish with lots of coriander leaves, topping with shredded cheese, pomegranate or chopped tomatoes. Serve steaming hot with a thick onion-tomato raita (made from hung curd) with a bit of chaat masala.

✨ Serving Tip: Serve with a thick onion-tomato raita made from hung curd, with a little chaat masala stirred in.
