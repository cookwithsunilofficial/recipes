Mumbai Meets Mexico (Signature Salad)

“Crunch, cream, and colour.”

A creamy salsa-style salad piled high on crisp nachos

⏱ Prep time
15 min
🔥 Cook time
None
🍽 Serves
2
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, finely chopped
  • A bunch of coriander leaves, finely chopped
  • 3 tsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp cheesy dip (optional)
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped jalapenos
  • 10–12 nachos

Method

1

Mix all the ingredients together in a bowl. Keep some coriander leaves aside to sprinkle on top.

2

Spread them on the nachos.

3

Sprinkle some more coriander leaves on top. Yummy salad is ready!

✨ Serving Tip: Assemble just before serving so the nachos stay shatteringly crisp under the salad.
