Stuffed Bafla Bati (Bharvan Batti)

“A taste of the Marwad.”

Marwad wheat dumplings, boiled then fried, stuffed with spiced peas

⏱ Prep time
30 min
🔥 Cook time
45 min
🍽 Serves
3–4
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian

Ingredients

For Batti

  • 1 cup whole wheat flour
  • 5 tsp ghee
  • 1/4 cup rava (semolina)
  • Salt as desired

For Stuffing

  • 1 boiled smashed potato (optional)
  • 1/2 cup boiled green peas
  • 1 crushed green chilli
  • A pinch of hing
  • 1/2 tsp split coriander seeds
  • 1/2 tsp jeera
  • 1/2 tsp fennel seeds (saunf)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • Salt as desired
  • 3 tsp oil

Method

1

For the batti, mix all the batti ingredients and make a dough.

2

Make round shapes from the dough as shown.

3

For the filling, heat oil in a pan, add jeera and let it splutter. Add coriander seeds and fennel seeds and roast, then add the rest of the ingredients and roast for about 3–4 minutes on a low flame.

4

The filling is ready. Allow it to cool for 15 minutes. Now take a circular dough piece and roll it out.

5

Fill the filling inside.

6

Make them circular again with the filling inside.

7

Boil about 3–4 cups of water and, when it comes to a boil, add the filled dough balls. Let them boil for 5 minutes — this is important, as they'll cook completely when we deep-fry them next.

💡 Pro Tip: Boiling before frying is what cooks the dough through — the deep-fry after is mainly for colour and crisp.
8

Take them onto a plate and let them dry for 5 minutes. Take enough ghee in a pan to deep-fry the dried batti.

9

Take them out on absorbent paper. Make sure they're golden brown on all sides. They absorb very little ghee — super healthy and delicious.

10

Break them, add ghee on top and serve with dal, garlic chutney, or have it like a samosa with sauce — the choice is yours!

✨ Serving Tip: Break them open, spoon over a little extra ghee, and serve with dal or garlic chutney.
