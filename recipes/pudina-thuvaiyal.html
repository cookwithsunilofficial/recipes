Pudina Thuvaiyal

“Fresh mint, big flavour.”

Fresh mint ground with roasted dals, garlic and tamarind

⏱ Prep time
10 min
🔥 Cook time
10 min
🍽 Serves
3–4
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup pudina (mint)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp urad dal
  • 1/2 tsp channa dal
  • 1–2 dry red chilli
  • 3–4 garlic cloves
  • A small piece of ginger
  • Salt as desired
  • A piece of imli (tamarind)
  • 1/4 tsp jeera
  • A pinch of asafoetida (hing)

Method

1

Take oil in a pan, add urad dal and channa dal and roast.

2

Add onion, ginger, garlic and dry red chilli and roast till the onions are slightly brown.

3

Add pudina and stir.

4

Add it to a grinder and grind. Your thuvaiyal is ready.

✨ Serving Tip: Lovely with hot rice and a spoon of sesame oil, or alongside curd rice.
