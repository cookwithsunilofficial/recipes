Onion-Tomato Chutney

“The tang that ties it together.”

Roasted onions and tomatoes, rich, tangy, finished with tadka

⏱ Prep time
10 min
🔥 Cook time
15 min
🍽 Serves
3–4
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 2 tsp oil
  • 2–3 medium onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp Bengal gram
  • 1/2 tsp urad dal, split
  • 2–3 garlic cloves
  • A piece of ginger
  • A piece of imli (tamarind)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1–2 dry red chilli
  • 1/4 tsp asafoetida (hing)
  • Salt as desired

Method

1

Take oil in a pan on medium heat and add the channa dal, then the urad dal, and roast.

2

Now add the onions, ginger, curry leaves and garlic and roast.

3

Now add the tomato, hing, dry red chilli and imli and roast.

💡 Pro Tip: Don’t add water when grinding — the tomatoes release enough of their own juice.
4

Put the mixture in a mixer, add red chilli powder and salt, and grind. Don't add water — the tomatoes release their juice. Garnish with a tadka of mustard seeds, dry red chilli and curry leaves.
