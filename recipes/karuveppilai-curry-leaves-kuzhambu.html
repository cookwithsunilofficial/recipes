Karuveppilai (Curry Leaves) Kuzhambu

“Grandma’s tangy secret.”

A tangy tamarind kuzhambu, simmered till the oil rises

⏱ Prep time
10 min
🔥 Cook time
20 min
🍽 Serves
4
🌱 Diet
Vegetarian
🌿

Ingredients

Ingredients Needed

  • Tamarind – small lemon-sized ball
  • Garlic – 1 pod (optional)
  • Salt as needed

Fry and Grind

  • Bengal gram (channa dal) – 1 tbsp
  • Urad dal – 1 tbsp
  • Coriander seeds – 1 tbsp
  • Whole black pepper – 1–2 tsp
  • Red chilli – 2
  • Curry leaves – 1 cup
  • (1:1 ratio for urad / coriander seeds / Bengal gram; 1:3 ratio for urad dal and curry leaves)

For the Seasoning

  • Sesame / sunflower oil – 3 tbsp
  • Mustard seeds – 1 tsp
  • Hing – a generous pinch
  • Fenugreek seeds – 1/2 tsp
  • A bunch of curry leaves

Method

1

Heat a tsp of oil and fry the Bengal gram dal, urad dal, coriander seeds, pepper and red chilli till the dal turns golden brown. Switch off the flame and add curry leaves, tossing them in the heat of the pan. Add a little water and grind everything to a smooth paste. Keep aside.

2

Soak tamarind in warm water, extract the juice and discard the pulp.

3

Heat 3 tbsp of sesame oil, add mustard seeds and, when they splutter, add fenugreek seeds, hing, garlic and curry leaves. Sauté for a few minutes.

4

Add the tamarind water, the salt needed and the ground paste. Simmer, cover with a lid, and cook until the kuzhambu thickens and the oil oozes out.

💡 Pro Tip: If no oil rises to the top, the oil was short — heat 2–3 tbsp more, add it in, and simmer covered. That sheen is the sign it’s done.
5

Tip: if you don't see the oil oozing out, the oil wasn't sufficient — heat 2–3 tbsp of oil in another pan, add it to the mixture, and simmer covered with a lid.

✨ Serving Tip: Serve with hot steamed rice and a spoon of ghee or sesame oil. The flavour deepens over a week in the fridge.
