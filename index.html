Recipes from a real kitchen, not a studio

I don't follow recipes to the letter — I use cookbooks for inspiration, for learning techniques and great flavour combinations. There are just too many variables in a home kitchen to get hung up on instructions. After a lot of experimentation and innovation, these are the recipes I've developed — easy to make, and nothing like something out of a MasterChef kitchen. I wish to share great food with everyone here. Hope you all like it!
